# Changes to Maven's own pages in this demonstration

The restructured site carries Maven's pages over as they are. These are the only changes made
to their text, each because it would otherwise show on a page of the new site.

| Page | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `guides/mini/guide-migration-to-mvn4.md` | Removed "TODO: list the few known plugins requiring 4.x, …" from the note on Maven 4-specific plugin releases. | A published TODO. |

Three flaws found in the review of maven.apache.org are gone without an edit, because the new
structure no longer shows the page they are on:

- "pay someone to explain this to you" in *Maven in 5 Minutes*: the page is replaced by the
  tutorial *Create your first project*, and its URL redirects there.
- "TODO: creating and using custom packaging" on the *Plugin Developers Centre*: its URL now
  redirects to the Extend section.
- The placeholder row "Upcoming: How to migrate plugins to the new Maven 4 API?" on the home
  page: the home page is new.
